# Privacy and security incident response

Status: beta operating procedure. Assign names, phone numbers, and alternates before real-patient use.

## Immediate actions

1. Stop or contain the exposure without destroying evidence. Disable a compromised account, revoke sessions, isolate the affected integration, or pause the relevant feature.
2. Preserve timestamps, audit records, provider notices, affected identifiers, and copies of relevant configuration. Do not place patient details in general chat or ticketing tools.
3. Notify the privacy officer and clinical owner immediately using the private contact route below.
4. Determine whether clinical availability or patient safety is affected and activate the downtime process if needed.

## Assessment record

Document:

- what happened and how it was discovered;
- dates and duration;
- systems, providers, and locations involved;
- types and sensitivity of information;
- number and identity of affected individuals;
- whether information was viewed, acquired, altered, lost, or unavailable;
- safeguards in place and evidence of misuse;
- containment and recovery actions;
- risk of harm and the rationale for notification decisions.

## Notifications

The privacy officer determines required notices under the Alberta HIA and any other applicable law. Where HIA’s risk-of-harm threshold is met, notify the OIPC, the Minister of Health, and affected individuals as required. Coordinate provider notices and preserve proof of when each notice was delivered.

Use the current official process at [OIPC breach notification](https://oipc.ab.ca/breach-notification/). Do not delay required notification while waiting for a perfect technical investigation.

## Recovery and follow-up

- remove unauthorized access and rotate affected secrets;
- restore from a verified backup when required;
- validate patient/practitioner access boundaries and data integrity;
- document corrective actions, owners, and due dates;
- update the PIA, agreements, training, and safeguards if the system or risk changed;
- retain the incident file under the approved retention schedule.

## Beta tabletop exercise

Before real-patient launch, rehearse this scenario: a setup link is forwarded to the wrong person and opened. Confirm who disables the link, how access is checked, what provider logs are available, who performs the HIA risk-of-harm assessment, and how the patient is contacted without adding sensitive details to email.
