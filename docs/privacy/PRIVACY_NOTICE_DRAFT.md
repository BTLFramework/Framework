## Information we collect

We collect the information needed to provide and support your care, including identity and contact information, date of birth, intake and follow-up answers, pain and function measures, recovery scores, assigned exercises, progress entries, reflections, messages, appointment-related information, and practitioner notes and assessments. We also process limited account and security information needed to operate the portal.

## Why we use it

We use this information to identify you, provide and document care, calculate and explain recovery measures, personalize assigned content, communicate with you, support continuity of care, protect the portal, troubleshoot problems, and meet professional and legal obligations. We limit collection, use, and disclosure to what is authorized and reasonably necessary for those purposes.

## Email and electronic communication

Ordinary email can carry privacy risks. Account-setup and password-reset emails are kept brief and do not include your recovery score, phase, or assessment details. View clinical details inside the signed-in portal. Contact the clinic if you prefer another available communication method.

## Who can see your information

Access is limited to you, your treating practitioner, authorized clinic personnel who need it for their role, and contracted service providers supporting the system. Information may also be disclosed where authorized or required by law. The clinic does not sell patient information.

## How we protect it

Safeguards include role-based access, same-patient access checks, encrypted network connections, password hashing, secure session cookies, rate limiting, restricted browser origins, minimized logs, and tested backups. No system can eliminate all risk; suspected incidents are assessed and handled under Alberta requirements.

## Your questions and access requests

You may contact the clinic privacy contact to ask how your information is handled or to request access or correction, subject to applicable law. You may also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta if you have a privacy concern.

Acknowledging this notice confirms that you had an opportunity to read it and ask questions. It is not a waiver of your privacy rights and is separate from consent to examination or treatment.
